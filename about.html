ABOUT 3AM FOUNDATION

A foundation built on compassion, remembrance and action.

The Aisha Abba Abdullahi Memorial Foundation (3AM Foundation) exists to turn remembrance into meaningful action by supporting people affected by blood-related health challenges and strengthening access to life-saving blood.

IN LOVING MEMORY

AISHA ABBA ABDULLAHI

OUR STORY

Aisha's memory became a mission to save lives.

The Aisha Abba Abdullahi Memorial Foundation was established in honour and memory of Aisha Abba Abdullahi.

Her memory reminds us of the value of life, the importance of compassion and the difference that timely support can make when someone is fighting for their life.

What began as remembrance has become a call to action — to create awareness, encourage voluntary blood donation, support access to blood and stand with individuals and families facing blood-related health challenges.

We honour a life by helping other lives have a better chance to continue.

OUR MISSION

Turning compassion into action.

To promote voluntary blood donation, increase awareness about blood-related health challenges, support access to life-saving blood and provide meaningful assistance to individuals and communities in need.

OUR VISION

A future where no life is lost for lack of blood.

We envision communities where people understand the importance of blood donation, blood is available when urgently needed, and people affected by blood-related conditions can access the support they need.

OUR OBJECTIVES

What we are committed to achieving.

Our work is focused on practical interventions that can create measurable impact in communities.

01

Promote Voluntary Blood Donation

Encourage more people to become regular voluntary blood donors and help build a stronger culture of blood donation.

02

Increase Blood Cancer Awareness

Educate communities about blood cancers, their impact, early awareness and the importance of supporting affected individuals and families.

03

Improve Access to Blood

Support systems and initiatives that make safe blood more accessible to patients who urgently need it.

04

Support Patients and Families

Stand with people facing blood-related health challenges by connecting them with appropriate support and resources.

05

Build Partnerships for Impact

Work with hospitals, blood banks, healthcare professionals, organisations, businesses and communities to create sustainable solutions.

06

Take Life-Saving Action

Move beyond awareness by developing practical programmes including blood drives, community outreach, education and other life-saving initiatives.
