WHO WE ARE

We believe access to blood should never be a matter of chance.

The Aisha Abba Abdullahi Memorial Foundation (3AM Foundation) exists to help build a culture where voluntary blood donation, awareness and timely access to blood become part of stronger communities.

Our work brings together advocacy, community mobilisation, blood donation campaigns, awareness initiatives and partnerships to support people whose lives may depend on access to blood.

OUR MISSION

Increase awareness, access and action.

OUR VISION

A society where no life is lost to blood scarcity.
