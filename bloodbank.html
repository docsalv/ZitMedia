THE VISION

A Blood Bank Built for Impact

The Aisha Abba Abdullahi Memorial Foundation (3AM Foundation) is seeking strategic partners, corporate sponsors, philanthropists, government agencies and grant organizations to support the establishment of a modern Blood Bank facility in Abuja.

The proposed facility will help improve access to safe, screened and adequately stored blood and blood components for patients, hospitals and emergency situations across Abuja and surrounding communities.

This initiative is part of the Foundation's broader commitment to reducing preventable deaths associated with inadequate access to safe blood.

Our Goal

To establish a sustainable, professionally managed blood bank that supports hospitals, patients, emergency responders and communities with safe and accessible blood supplies.

What We Plan To Achieve

Project Objectives

The proposed Blood Bank is designed to create a reliable infrastructure for blood collection, screening, storage and distribution.

Purpose-Built Facility

Establish a modern medical-grade facility in Abuja designed to support safe blood collection, processing, storage and distribution.

Safe Blood Screening

Introduce appropriate modern screening systems for transfusion-transmissible infections and other required blood safety procedures.

Reliable Cold Chain

Provide dependable refrigeration, power backup and cold-chain infrastructure to protect blood products from collection to distribution.

Rapid Distribution

Strengthen access to blood for hospitals, emergency response units and patients who urgently need transfusion support.
