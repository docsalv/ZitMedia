THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE MISSION

Meet the people making a difference.

Behind every life touched by 3AM Foundation is a team of committed people, professionals, leaders and partners working together to strengthen blood access, advance awareness and create lasting community impact.

OUR TEAM

People with a purpose.

Our team brings together passionate professionals and dedicated individuals who contribute their experience, skills and commitment to advancing the mission of 3AM Foundation.

FOUNDER / CEO

HUM. Huseina Baba-Inna FHSID

As the devoted caregiver to her late daughter, Aisha, the Founder of 3AM Foundation transformed her personal journey of love, resilience, and loss into a mission dedicated to improving lives and supporting families in need.

She is also a Fellow in Humanitarian and Social Development, committed to advancing compassionate, sustainable solutions that create meaningful impact in communities.

HEAD OF ADMINISTRATION

Dr. Hassan Baba-Inna

As the Founder’s brother who stood beside her throughout the fight for their late Aisha, he now serves as Head of Administration, bringing his experience as an educationist and his deep passion for research to the mission of 3AM Foundation.

ADMINISTRATIVE SECRETARY

Hyeldai Dada Ulea

With a BSc in Microbiology and a passion for cancer research, he serves as the Secretary, overseeing the Foundation’s day-to-day activities and ensuring that programmes, communications, and operations are carried out effectively.

FINANCIAL SECRETARY

Abdulwahab Baba-Inna

Responsible for supporting the Foundation’s financial administration, maintaining proper financial records and promoting accountability in the management of organisational resources.

MEDIA

Salvation Sarki

Leads the Foundation’s media and communications efforts, helping to amplify its mission, programmes, stories and public awareness initiatives across digital platforms.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT LEAD

Reedwan Abo

Supports community engagement initiatives by connecting the Foundation with communities, stakeholders and beneficiaries while helping strengthen participation in its programmes.
