THE VISION

Bringing Blood Donation Closer to the People

The Aisha Abba Abdullahi Memorial Foundation (3AM Foundation) is seeking strategic partners, corporate sponsors, philanthropists and organizations to support the acquisition and deployment of a fully equipped Mobile Blood Donation Van.

The mobile unit will enable the Foundation and its partners to take organized blood donation campaigns directly to communities, workplaces, schools, religious institutions, markets, corporate organizations and public events.

Rather than waiting for willing donors to visit a fixed facility, the mobile blood donation unit will help bring the opportunity to donate blood closer to them.

Our Mission

To make voluntary blood donation more accessible by creating a mobile platform capable of supporting organized blood collection campaigns across Abuja and, eventually, other parts of Nigeria.
